HOW TO OPEN THE TRUSTLED AI WEBSITE
====================================

FIRST: unzip this folder. Do not open files from inside the zip — the page
links will not work until the files are extracted to a real folder.
  - Windows: right-click the zip -> "Extract All"
  - Mac: double-click the zip

THEN, pick ONE of these:

--- OPTION 1: Just look at it (simplest) ---
Double-click "index.html". The site opens in your browser. Click around the
navigation (Services, Solutions, About, etc.) — every page is included.

If links seem not to work this way, use Option 2 instead.

--- OPTION 2: Run it like a real website (most reliable) ---
This starts a small local web server so every link and page behaves exactly
as it will once deployed. You need Python installed (most Macs already have it).
  - Windows: double-click "start-windows.bat"
  - Mac:     double-click "start-mac-linux.command"
A browser tab opens at http://localhost:8000 showing the site. Keep the little
black window open while you browse; close it when you are done.

--- OPTION 3: Put it live on the internet (for real use) ---
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag the WHOLE unzipped folder onto the page.
3. You get a live web address in under a minute. Every link works.
   The contact form starts capturing messages once it is live here.
